# Manual criterion tournament — Rename the Planet

Proxy judge (deepseek-v4.1-flash-free via the local 9router gateway) was DOWN
during this build: 502 "fetch connect timeout" on all phase-1 batches, then a
60 s timeout on a trivial test call at 09:32 UTC. So the tournament below is a
manual one, scored by hand against the house judge's published criteria, in
the order the judge weighs them:

1. Names a solar-system planet + funny new name + why in the same line
   (pass/fail — all 24 candidates pass; Pluto deliberately excluded, it is not
   a planet, and cheap Uranus name-puns / Mars-bar / Lord-of-the-Rings
   wordplay deliberately excluded: the brief says the laugh must come from the
   IDEA, not a pun on the planet's real name alone).
2. Funny: name + why land together as a joke on the first read.
3. Why earns the name: leans on that planet's own traits, would not fit any
   other planet.
4. Fresh: not the obvious pun anyone would reach for.
5. Tight: every word doing work.

## Phase 3 — wording variants of the winner

- V1: "Rename Jupiter 'Pimple': one zit wider than Earth that has refused to
  pop for 300 years." (88 chars)
- V2: "Rename Jupiter 'Pimple': a zit wider than Earth that has refused to pop
  for 300 years." (86 chars)
- V3: "Rename Jupiter 'Pimple': one zit bigger than Earth that won't pop after
  300 years." (84 chars)
- V4: "Rename Jupiter 'Pimple': a zit wider than Earth, festering for 300
  years, that refuses to pop." (89 chars)

V1 beats V2: "one zit" stresses the absurd singularity (ONE zit, wider than
Earth). V1 beats V3: "refused to pop" personifies the zit (it refuses) and
"wider" is more precise than "bigger"; "has refused ... for 300 years"
stresses the duration. V1 beats V4: V4 buries the punch mid-sentence.

FINAL LINE (V1): Rename Jupiter 'Pimple': one zit wider than Earth that has
refused to pop for 300 years.
Character count: 88 (limit 200). One line, names Jupiter, gives the funny new
name "Pimple", and the why (Great Red Spot, a storm wider than Earth, raging
~300 years) in the same breath.
